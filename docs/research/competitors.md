# Hishab Sathi competitor and customer research (SSOT §14 Q1/Q2)

Filled during Phase 0. Sources: public websites and app listings; pricing not confirmed until vendor interviews.

## Competitors found

| # | Product | Market | Notes |
|---|---------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Hishabee (hishabee.io) | Bangladesh | Digital ledger app for small business; instant customer add by name/phone; positions itself as a digital ledger/billing app. Pricing: not confirmed from public pages. |
| 2 | Takvaro (Google Play: com.takvaro.app) | Bangladesh | Simple shop ledger ("hisab khata"); records daily sales and expenses, baki khata per party. Pricing: not confirmed. |
| 3 | Khatabook (khatabook.com) | India | Dominant regional digital khata; 5Cr+ businesses claimed; free app, payment reminders, invoices, inventory. Not Bangladesh-focused. |
| 4 | DigiKhata (digikhata.pk) | Pakistan | Party ledger, bookkeeping, payments, financing; DigiQR, PIN lock. Not Bangladesh. |

## What to confirm in the 10 shop-owner interviews (SSOT §18, item 4)

Q1: Which of the above (or others) do distributors in the target market actually use?
Q2: Paper khata, Excel, or app today — and what happened the last time records were wrong or lost?
Q3: What do they pay, if anything, for a khata app?
Q4: Do they track consignment (goods left at shops) separately from sales?
